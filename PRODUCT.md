# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Dental laboratories and their owners, supervisors, and teams in Chile are the primary MVP audience. They evaluate the service while coordinating cases, files, production status, and payments across tools such as Excel, WhatsApp, email, and phones. Independent dental technicians and clinics with in-house laboratories are secondary audiences; their needs must not dilute the landing page's primary positioning around dental laboratories.

## Product Purpose

Comelu is a software product in development that will help dental laboratories organize work orders, case files, production status, payments, and receipts in one place. Its future authenticated application will be a product UI for this operational work. This public marketing landing page is a brand surface: it explains the value proposition, earns trust, and converts qualified visitors to the waitlist for early access and product-validation conversations.

## Positioning

Comelu is a specialized operational workspace for dental laboratory work in Chile. It connects the order, its clinical files, its current production state, and its payment context so a laboratory can follow each case without reconstructing the story from scattered messages and spreadsheets.

## Operating Context

The work is case-based and time-sensitive. Teams may pass work between people, receive incomplete clinical information, and need to find photos, scans, instructions, payment records, and ownership quickly. The public site is Spanish (Chile) and speaks to visitors considering a future operational tool, not a general-purpose productivity app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The landing page currently collects waitlist interest through a validated form protected by Turnstile.
- The product is not yet publicly available; do not imply shipped functionality, customers, metrics, pricing, integrations, or outcomes that have not been confirmed.
- The current public scope describes work orders, case files, operational status, payments, receipts, and potential future automation.
- Do not position Comelu primarily as an AI product. Lead with operational clarity, traceability, coordination, and control. Automation is a supporting capability, not the core promise.
- Preserve the existing waitlist conversion goal and Spanish (Chile) content.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: precisa, moderna y confiable.
- The public marketing landing page is a brand surface. The future authenticated Comelu application is a product UI; do not apply landing-page persuasion patterns as though they define the operational application.
- Directional references: Linear's precision and hierarchy; Stripe's storytelling and composition; Vercel's technical elegance; Apple's calm, premium restraint; modern dental CAD/CAM equipment; and premium technical packaging and laboratory instruments.
- Comelu should be warmer and more grounded in dental laboratory work than a generic technology company. References guide attributes, not imitation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product and waitlist copy live in `src/App.tsx`.
- The public page metadata and structured data live in `index.html`.
- Existing brand imagery lives in `src/assets/landing/hero/` and `src/assets/illustrations/`.
- No confirmed testimonials, customer logos, case studies, benchmark metrics, pricing, licensing terms, or deployment claims are available in the repository. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make the value legible quickly for people who know the daily friction of laboratory work.
2. Build trust through specificity, clarity, and factual restraint.
3. Keep the path to the waitlist direct and low-friction.
4. Treat the dental case as the shared operational context for files, progress, and payment information.
5. Prefer calm precision over generic software polish or speculative technology claims.
6. Lead with operational clarity, traceability, coordination, and control; describe automation only as supporting or future capability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain AA-compliant text and UI contrast, semantic HTML, logical heading order, keyboard navigation with visible focus, screen-reader labels for controls and forms, accessible validation and error messages, non-color-only meaning, 44 by 44 px touch targets where practical, responsive behavior from mobile through large desktop, and functionality at 200% browser zoom. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and never be required to understand or use the page.
